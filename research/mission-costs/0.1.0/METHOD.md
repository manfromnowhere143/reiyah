# Accounting boundaries and retained failures

Version `0.1.0`. All reported accounting is retrospective and exploratory.

## Physical events and copied timers

The [core plan](PLAN.md) binds an explicit source inventory before each
reconciliation. Immutable copies of receipt inputs make later live-log appends
unable to alter a closed snapshot. The original core snapshot remains private;
the published second snapshot includes subsequent research and eleven push
receipts. Its freeze digest is in [sources.json](sources.json).

One STARTED/COMPLETED pair is one outer process. One physical download ledger
line is one request. Reused receipt ID `0036` identifies two distinct transfers
with different payload paths; both are charged. Deduplicating content reports
storage identity, not a reduced resource charge. Headers, outbound requests,
TLS, Git transfers and unmetered browser/search traffic are outside retained
response-body measurements. Reused historical images, metadata and runtimes
have no fabricated acquisition cost in this mission.

Exact decimal tokens retain recorded monotonic durations. Integer-microsecond
UTC endpoints support a separate interval union. Their sums can differ slightly
because the clocks and recording boundaries differ. Neither is CPU time.
Category-specific unions can overlap and are not additive. Category names are
a frozen naming heuristic: for example some `-verify-` and `research-check`
stages fall under setup/diagnostics. Exact event names and times remain visible;
the labels are not a labor allocation or a performance benchmark.

Internal matching/search/proof timers, review copies and published snapshots
are references to existing work. Inference-call intervals must nest inside
their exact export launch and match every allocated model/image call. They
are never added to their containing process a second time. Duration-only
reader/tool records have no invented timestamps. Their scalar sums are listed
separately and cannot be used to extend the measured interval union.

## Additional receipts and finite cutoff

The [first supplement](supplement.json) preserves nullable-list reader probes,
the failed ENOSPC launcher and result-view tool observations, the cached audit,
envelope timers and losslessly archived synthetic outputs. Separate checking
reads every one of the 59 archive members and verifies its original bytes.
No actual prediction packet, frozen input or reserved data was archived away.

The [final appendix](final-appendix.json) binds the operating-witness search
and verification outputs, all 185 state records and 196 new proof records.
Its 1,676 timer references include repeated endpoint/proof values and published
aliases; they are not 1,676 additional processes. The five additional outer
intervals are the explicit second-snapshot launcher, core reconciliation and
verification, CSV newline normalization and CSV rational-field correction.
The combined interval union is checked with a separate endpoint sweep.

The appendix's own creation/check, this publication, subsequent consistency
checks and final push happen after its input cutoff. Their ordinary receipts
continue in the private closeout. An accounting artifact cannot contain its
own completed publication interval without a later artifact. The final
closeout retains that finite tail without an endless chain of republished
totals. Neither public cutoff is represented as total mission cost.

## Failure preservation

All 21 failed interval-bearing attempts remain individually identified in
the core ledger. They include five HTTP/transport failures and sixteen local
process/control failures. Deliberately rejected bad fixtures inside passing
test suites are not failed production attempts; failed test-suite invocations
are retained as failures. HTTP 200 responses with JSON error objects remain
separate from transport success and do not count as admitted sources.

The initial nullable fixed-list reads and attempted schema overrides failed.
The corrected footer-only view preserves all prediction data bytes; both
decoder paths then agree on every cached row. Failed metadata writer and
integer-type preservation controls remain archived with their inputs.

The storage incident occurs before a verification child can start or write
its STARTED receipt. It remains a tool-only failure, not a missing successful
verification. A verified lossless archive of completed synthetic output copies
permits a fresh successful check. A later observation of more free disk space
has no established causal attribution to that small recovery operation.

The first outcome inventory invocation had a wrong relative child path and
executed no audit. Its absolute-path retry passes. A later cross-table audit
detects dictionary-formatted anchor thresholds in the new public witness CSV.
Commit `24a895ee5198b35f9efecbfc546aa59144b0939c` corrects only serialization
and the exporter. The first publication, both CSV hashes, failed audit and
successful complete-row/exporter readback remain retained. No scientific
outcome, reference bound or input membership is relabeled.

## Limits and reproduction

Uninstrumented coding, reading, research, review and routine orchestration are
not fully enumerated. Not every executor tool duration was transcribed into
the native receipt ledger. Human inspection/adjudication, prices, billing,
electricity, hardware depreciation, historical preparation and customer delays
remain unknown. No compute-only ratio is full-cost savings.

The cost controls are runnable here with
`python -B -m unittest -v test_costs test_cost_supplement`.
For an authorized retained packet, `cost_collect.py --root ROOT --freeze
FREEZE --output NEW_DIRECTORY` reconstructs a new report from an existing
snapshot, and `cost_verify.py` checks its exact sources and arithmetic.
Supplement and appendix tools retain their own explicit frozen inventories.
Do not alter a closed freeze or source projection to make a replay pass.

The [original outcome inventory](outcomes.json) is bound to `cc59ec6` and
the [follow-up inventory](followup-outcomes.json) to corrected `24a895e`.
They inspect already published scalar rows only. No image, withheld outcome
or new model output is accessed, and no scientific result is rerun unchanged.
