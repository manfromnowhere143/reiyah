# Distribution boundary

Document ID: reiyah.public-motion.distribution. Version 0.1.0.
Lifecycle status: exploratory. Date: 20 September 2026.

Original code, synthetic controls and original mathematical/procedural writing
in this packet are Apache-2.0. No NGSIM implementation is copied. The public
packet distributes source metadata, hashes and aggregate research findings;
the official metadata, PDF, seed records, trajectories and derived per-case
coordinates remain in the owned private source/result store.

Source attribution: U.S. Department of Transportation Federal Highway
Administration (2016), *Next Generation Simulation (NGSIM) Vehicle Trajectories
and Supporting Data*, provided by ITS DataHub through Data.transportation.gov,
DOI 10.21949/1504477, accessed 20 September 2026. The PDF also names the Research
Data Exchange Program and Cambridge Systematics contributors to collection and
processing. No source-provider endorsement is claimed.

Source declarations conflict between CC BY-SA 3.0, CC BY-SA 4.0 and older RDE
redistribution language. They are retained and unresolved for payload
redistribution. Reiyah's Apache license does not replace source terms or
relicense data-derived material. No private address, route destination or
personal attribute is inferred from these vehicle identifiers.

Public summaries describe this bounded research and its admission limits.
A new recipient must obtain the named source bytes through an appropriate
source route and verify hashes to reproduce actual-source calculations.
Synthetic controls run without any third-party data. No legal compliance,
physical safety, independent replication or commercial permission is asserted.
