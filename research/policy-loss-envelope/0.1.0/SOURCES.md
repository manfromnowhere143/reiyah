# Inherited prediction and curve evidence

Version `0.1.0`, 18 September 2026. This study consumes the already verified
[complete nominal detector curves](../../operating-policy/0.1.0/detector-curves.csv)
and their retained source/verification identities. It acquires no additional
data and reads no image or annotation payload.

The preceding [operating-policy study](../../operating-policy/0.1.0/README.md)
enumerates all 299 old and 220 new threshold cells under strict filtering of
retained scores above 0.25. It verifies the complete image/score joins,
matching results and native proofs. The [original qualification record](../../public-predictions/0.1.0/SOURCES.md)
binds the local YOLO11n/YOLO26n checkpoints, runtime, settings, exact image
membership and export states. The source is one model family with no genuine
three-revision history; this envelope does not meet that acquisition target.

The comparison has 64 already exposed camera images and 305 eligible projected
car references. The publisher's 3D projection convention is an explicit proxy,
not tight visible-image truth. Same-image/scene dependence and the preceding
reference/timing uncertainty remain. This study does not access the separate
1,433-image outcome reserve or perform another detector export.

The 106-binding freeze records the exact source CSV, preceding summaries and
scope, original source conditions, implementation, controls and selected Python
runtime. [summary.json](summary.json) publishes its identity, source-curve hash
and exact result/checker hashes. Numerical curve and threshold coverage,
FP/FN/rank identities and original unit-loss minima are checked again because
they are inputs to a different mathematical claim. Earlier experiments are
not rerun unchanged.

The scientific source conditions are inherited in
[DISTRIBUTION.md](DISTRIBUTION.md). Local disk exhaustion prevented one
verification process from launching; recovery and the successful fresh
verification are retained in [COSTS.md](COSTS.md). No source bytes or frozen
scientific inputs were removed during recovery. Completed synthetic outputs
were moved into a lossless, individually verified private archive.
