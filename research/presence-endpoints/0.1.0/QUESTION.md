## Decision and hypothesis

Decide whether a small research-only conversion is justified, or whether this
route should be terminated as redundant or semantically wrong.

H1: under the exact guard below, the original and converted families have the
same possible object sets and paired losses. The existing endpoint checker
therefore supplies exact bounds while the old full-world path can hit its cap.
An independently implemented conventional endpoint calculation must agree.
Agreement would demonstrate reuse of existing mathematics/software, not a new
solver, physical conclusion, statistical claim or competitor superiority.

Guard: use only validated authored synthetic legacy cases; observed base and
additions; finite references; no model clauses; no conditional edges. Each object
is either always present or conditioned on one positive Boolean variable. Each
such variable occurs in at most one object globally. Unused declared variables
may be dropped. Nonnegative penalties, normalized nonnegative weights and shared
base identities remain the original contract's requirements.

A mandatory object becomes a hypothetical derived premise in the study request,
with its evidence digest bound to the original authored case and conversion
rule. It is not a human observation or a supplied external record. Optional
objects are free deletions, with a global budget admitting all of them. Reset
the converted Boolean model only after recording and checking the complete
mapping. Bind a new reference-context identity to the original case and the
conversion rule. Never preserve a stale context digest across this change.

The proof of family equality is a bijection between optional object-presence
bits and their deletion indicators. The unique-variable restriction matters:
correlated objects cannot be made independent silently. Even where endpoint
bounds might coincide under a wider relaxation, this study does not claim
family equality for that relaxation.
