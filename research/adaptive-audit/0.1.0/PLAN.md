# Adaptive audit comparison
Document ID: reiyah.adaptive-audit.plan. Version 0.1.0. Status: exploratory.
Date: 19 September 2026.

## Question and frozen allocation
Does published adaptive betting materially improve observation effort on a relevant
Reiyah finite-population decision, compared with a fixed stake and conventional
exact stopping? Existing perception results and the earlier authored study are
already exposed. This is a new frozen exploratory method comparison on those
operands, not a new empirical validation population or a claim of novelty.

Freeze the implementation, this plan, mathematics, source ledger, inputs,
sampling orders, checker, controls, supervisor and Python runtime before running
any new stopping outcomes. Include all twelve populations: four authored six-unit
controls (positive, negative, exact tie, residual interval); five authored 64-unit
populations (constant positive/negative half-margin, binary tie, 48-positive/16-negative
binary margin, and a quarter-width interval around zero); and three 64-image
populations from the closed loss-tradeoff packet (exact projection, simultaneous
translation radius 5, and one arbitrary reference edit per image).
The three previously exposed reference conditions have known aggregate signs;
their inclusion covers useful and unresolved decisions without choosing a favorable
new stopping result. No parameters are fitted on this allocation.

Use every permutation of each six-unit population. For each larger population,
freeze 128 uniform-without-replacement permutations from Python Random, with seed
SHA256("reiyah.adaptive-audit.v1/" + population ID + "/" + replicate index),
interpreted as a big-endian integer. Retain exact generated orders and runtime
binding. These are reproducible diagnostic simulations; 128 paths do not certify
coverage or provide an exact expected-query estimate. No iid image assumption is
needed by the finite-population theorem. Unit querying is simulated, not newly
performed human work.

## Information and methods
Every procedure knows the complete membership and pre-observation count bound B_i.
For perception, B_i = number of eligible predictions in A plus B; the old-minus-new
equal-unit-penalty matching-loss difference lies in [-B_i,B_i] regardless of
reference count. For authored units B_i=1. Querying reveals both previously
retained lower/upper endpoints under the declared reference family, not physical
truth. Querying a zero-bound unit is unnecessary: it remains in membership with
known zero contribution. No unknown or nonresponse is imputed to a point value.

Registered statistical arms are fixed_half, alpha_d10 and alpha_d100. All use the
same uniform orders, range normalization, two directions, exact stopping and
error allocation. ALPHA follows Stark's published predictable truncated-shrinkage
construction with eta0=(1+mu0)/2, d=10 or 100, and c=(eta0-mu0)/2. The positive
epsilon uses a declared 24-bit downward rational approximation to c/sqrt(d+t);
there is no floating-point decision arithmetic. These are declared implementations,
not a replication of the paper's experiments or author software. Retain both
settings without selecting a post-hoc winner or combining their tests.

Conventional comparisons: exact interval stopping on every identical order and
a single descending-B_i order (stable identity tie break). The latter receives
the same prior information. Statistical and exact all-world guarantees differ;
an apparent statistical query saving cannot be reported as an exact certificate
or a special Reiyah capability.

Each fixed population and separately chosen statistical arm has total risk <=1/20,
split 1/40 per direction, threshold 40. There is no simultaneous claim over
populations or arms. Exact sign: support if full lower mean>0; exclusion if full
upper mean<=0; otherwise unresolved. A statistical exclusion certifies a strictly
negative upper mean; exact stopping also handles equality. Full census always
returns the residual interval result. A simultaneous statistical conflict is
unresolved. Record first stopping point and continue all prefixes only for diagnostics.

## Verification
A checker importing no producer reconstructs every path from frozen orders,
all factors, support positivity, null conditional drift, first stopping points,
exact intervals, trace digests and summaries. It independently derives the source
operands from byte-bound prior components/counts and checks every retained
endpoint's agreement with its parent context. It exact-binds the completed parent
geometry proof and verification; it does not claim to rerun those closed proofs.
Small allocations enumerate all orders and exact false-crossing/decision probability.
For large allocations report observed diagnostic error counts, never infer coverage
from zero errors. Relevant controls cover invalid ranges/statuses/membership,
changed source/plan, singular conditional means, equality, forged orders/wealth,
traces/stops and post-freeze tampering. Failures remain retained.

## Resource and custody bounds
At most twelve populations, 128 sampled orders for each large population, three
statistical arms, 600 cumulative outer-process seconds execution and 600 checking
(including failed attempts and retries), and 128 MiB of new public/private study
artifacts. This explicitly enlarges the completed predecessor's small-fixture
bounds to admit the 64-unit comparison. No prior phase budget is reset.
Stop below 5 GiB free storage. Source/controls/packaging/publication are separate
timed categories. The supervisor rejects an unfinished prior START without receipt,
enters network denial before the pinned outcome/check runtime starts, preserves
partial output and terminates the owned process group on its remaining time cap.
Private source acquisition is bounded to six direct article retrievals, each <=8 MiB;
the first failed HTTP receipt is retained, not overwritten or silently retried.

Per-image operands, geometry and empirical path traces remain private. Publish
authored inputs/traces and aggregate derived results with inherited nuScenes terms.
No reserved image, model inference/training, dependencies, paid compute, outreach
or deployment. Preserve controller and Engine source. Record known process/CPU
time and bytes, separate nested timers, source reuse and unmeasured human/economic
cost. Safe normal push and exact publisher readback are authorized; Gate A remains
operator-unaccepted.
