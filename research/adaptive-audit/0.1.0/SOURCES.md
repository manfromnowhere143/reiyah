# Primary methods and scope
Document ID: reiyah.adaptive-audit.sources. Version 0.1.0.
Review date: 19 September 2026. Metadata, exact versions, private body digests,
access and redistribution boundaries are in [sources.json](sources.json).

[ALPHA, Stark, 2201.02707v9, 12 August 2022](https://arxiv.org/abs/2201.02707v9)
is the selected comparator. Its bounded finite-population and predictable
alternative construction fits the frozen endpoint target. This implementation
uses declared shrinkage settings and rational epsilon rounding; it does not
reproduce the paper's election experiments or copy its implementation.
The source's equation 14 has an inconsistent summation subscript; section 3
explicitly identifies the previous-observation sum used here.

[Adaptive auditing of AI systems, Zhou et al., 2605.07002v1, 7 May 2026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.07002v1)
tests subgroup performance and an adaptive auditor's ability to find failures.
Its global-robustness interpretation adds an asymptotically capable search
assumption. That does not provide a finite-budget global-robustness claim for
our fixed perception population. The full HTML body and its CC BY 4.0 header
are retained; its separate metadata retrieval returned HTTP 406 and is retained
as failed. No retry bypassed that response.

[Sequential Audit Sampling, Kato and Nakagawa, 2604.06116v2, 13 August 2026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.06116v2)
uses binary finite-population counts and an indifference region, with exact
hypergeometric recursion or separately calibrated simulation. Changing our
continuous loss endpoints to that binary contract would change the question,
so it is contextual rather than a matched comparator.

The earlier [primary audit](../../roadmap-audit/0.1.0/FRONTIER.md) retains RLFA,
prediction-assisted inference and the wider comparison. This narrow update
does not establish an exhaustive 2026 frontier review. Three full HTML method
reviews and five successful body/metadata retrievals supply the present source
basis; one failed retrieval and one earlier malformed discovery URL remain.
Paper reading, agreement and successful local checking do not establish novelty,
independent replication, real observation savings, safety or customer value.
