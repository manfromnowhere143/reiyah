# Retain the sampled contract; qualify native behavior before trials

Document ID: `reiyah.nuplan-outcome-source.decision`. Version: `0.1.0`.
Lifecycle status: `exploratory`. 16 October 2026.

The selected nuPlan v1.2 source supplies explicit outcome, clock and termination
semantics for a bounded simulator investigation. Retain that candidate. Do not
admit physical recovery labels, human readiness, empirical performance or a trial
population on the strength of source inspection.

The consequential decision is whether a collision-free result covers its declared
observation window, rather than merely reflecting missing output, a shorter
history, an at-fault subset or successful program execution. Ordinary direct
inspection is the conventional comparator in this audit. No reduced-work,
superiority or new safety-method claim was tested.

The next authorized step is a prospectively bounded authored source-fixture check
of the pinned collision and termination paths. Freeze the cases, expected event
sets, timestamp vectors, native entry points, exact dependencies, restrictions
and resource budget before running. Include a positive collision, rear/stopped-ego
contacts outside the at-fault subset, repeated track contact, the final scenario
point, disabled metrics and execution/callback failures. No dataset trials, model
inference or full simulator rollout is needed for that check.

Require agreement with a separate conventional calculation under equal information.
Record contradictory behavior and stop; do not change the endpoint or weaken a
failure expectation to obtain agreement. Static custody controls in this packet
are not those native controls. The upstream test names are discovery evidence,
not executed positive controls or a measured event population.

Only after native qualification should a new protocol consider trial acquisition.
It must freeze a meaningful simulator-only comparison, its population and common
window, complete run/metric/history obligations, all failure/exclusion states,
access terms and resources. Code availability alone does not qualify any existing
saved trial corpus. Missing physical clock and annotation uncertainty remain
unqualified even if the simulator contract passes.

The TD2D and manD documentation routes and exposed microscopy comparisons remain
closed. No readiness modeling, unrelated stream expansion or outreach follows
from this decision. The full HARBOR mission remains intact; broad product expansion
is stopped pending demonstrated value. **Gate A remains operator-unaccepted.**
