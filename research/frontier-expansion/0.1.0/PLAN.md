# Frozen relational-contract development study

Document ID: reiyah.frontier-expansion.plan. Version 0.1.0.
Status: exploratory. Literature and the public benchmark's published outcomes
were already visible during design. All new execution cases are authored;
their expected outcomes are specified in advance, not held-out performance data.

Freeze all 20 allocated cases and all 24 world alternatives in allocation.json,
format gate, both arithmetic paths, execution/checking code, controls, method,
plan, runtime digest and supervisor before the allocated execution. Record the
freeze digest in results and verification. No source model, raw image, new
prediction, human judgment or reserved outcome is evaluated.

The question is whether a small exact offline checker can preserve common-world
actor/clock alternatives and correctly distinguish complete, contradicted,
partial, missing and interpolation-unqualified clearance obligations. Allocate
boundary equality, rational strict violation, ego acceleration/deceleration,
missing actor/lead/ego, temporal incompleteness, unspecified interpolation,
clock and identity ambiguity, an interior asynchronous violation, empty worlds,
disjoint horizons, correlated coordinates and different-world violation times.
Retain every case and any failure. No speed/accuracy/savings superiority is tested.

Controls include these authored cases, 18 malformed-input rejection families,
eight altered-result/input bindings, two JSON parser rejections and an exhaustive
3^6 by 2 by 3 grid (4,374 checks) spanning endpoint values, interpolation modes
and clock shifts. The checker shares only the format gate and canonical digest;
its segment-pair arithmetic and reduction do not call the producer. This is
same-session internal verification, not independent replication.

Limits:128 worlds/case,256 samples/trace,canonical rational numerator magnitude
and denominator at most 10^18. Cumulative execution and checking are each capped
at 600 outer-process seconds, including failed/retried attempts. Other commands
have 120 seconds each. The owned supervisor denies networking before offline
runtime startup, rejects unfinished prior receipts, caps new artifacts at
128 MiB excluding the checkout, and stops growth at 5 GiB free. Preserve failures
and issue a new explicit freeze if frozen code needs correction.

Public-source qualification is descriptive, separately from the experiment.
Inspect the pinned Drive the Thoughts benchmark index and only the first
alphabetically listed scene's two JSON sidecars. Do not open media or execute
upstream code. Record observed fields, rights and discrepancies; absence in that
scope cannot establish absence in the entire repository. Its annotations and
model outputs are not used as clearance truth or as an accuracy test here.

Keep all 1,433 reserved images closed, prior negatives discoverable, controller
and closed candidates unchanged, Gate A operator-unaccepted and perception core
unchanged. Publish only authored code/fixtures and reviewed summaries/source
metadata; all retrieved third-party bodies stay private. Normal guarded
integration/push and exact publisher readback remain authorized.
