## Fixed decision

On the complete delivered pair of220unique route records, does the artifact
labelled UniAD-Base have strictly larger mean recorded driving score than
UniAD-Tiny AND a nonnegative difference in recorded success rate in EVERY
reported scenario group? A negative group difference rejects this proposed
blanket non-regression rule even if the overall mean improves. Zero is allowed
for group non-regression; zero is not strict aggregate improvement.

This is a hypothetical research screening rule: prefer Base for further work
only if both conditions hold; otherwise investigate the preserved regressing
groups. It is not a publisher rule, customer release criterion or safety claim.
No threshold, group or missing-data policy may be tuned after outcomes.
