# Forensic value verdict

Document ID: `reiyah.value-verdict`. Version: `0.1.0`.
Date: 21 September 2026. Lifecycle status: `exploratory`.
Review base: `948353a21872ebdf323e5c07a8f845124669a645`.

## Verdict

**STOP broad technical and company-scale investment in the present Reiyah
evidence-engine thesis.** Preserve the sound research code, exact negative
results and source records, but do not add another solver, sensing module,
simulation layer or HARBOR domain until one real revision workflow demonstrates
decision value or lower complete cost against the owner's competent method.

The specific investment rejected here is continued autonomous expansion of
Reiyah as a differentiated product for autonomous-system revision decisions on
the strength of the current repository evidence. This is not a finding that the
software is incorrect or that all future uses are impossible. It is a decision
that the current evidence does not justify more product research of the same
kind.

The strongest supported contribution is narrower:

> Reiyah is a reproducible offline research implementation that keeps declared
> joint reference alternatives, missing states and conditional premises explicit,
> and emits proof payloads that a separate checker can challenge within small,
> versioned mathematical families.

That contribution is useful research infrastructure. Its mathematical pieces
are established matching, interval, optimization, partial-identification and
sequential-inference techniques. The composition has caught real input and
protocol defects, but every direct competent comparison retained here is parity,
a Reiyah disadvantage, or an unresolved physical/commercial question. No
defensible frontier or commercial moat follows.

## Why the starting MODIFY assessment changed

The earlier MODIFY posture was appropriate while a practical advantage remained
untested. The accumulated tests now point consistently in the other direction:

- In the four-arm perception study, the equally informed conventional arm ties
  the best Reiyah query histories. The competent baseline reaches every one of
  the 99 retrospective resolved query floors; the alternative selector records
  zero wins and four losses. See the [value-disproof result](../../value-disproof/0.1.0/README.md)
  at commit `7dd7d5f4c7f7c2f8319663a99ed2d910928ab9d1`.
- Checked certificate reuse has median observed time about 0.529 seconds versus
  0.327 seconds for full computation on its retained small assay. It reuses 70
  certificates and recomputes ten, but the checking overhead dominates. See
  [the revision report](../../../docs/PERCEPTION_REVISION_AUDIT_2026-09-16.md).
- The sequential study validates its specified finite procedures, but the proxy
  helps once, hurts twice, and its sole control-variate saving is about 0.005415
  expected queries. A conventional exact order needs two queries on that case.
  See [sequential audit](../../sequential-audit/0.1.0/README.md) at commit
  `2e480622a59ad158ff9234af178f2d195e78ad68`.
- Complete measurement-response partitions, native-grid correction, mapping,
  presence endpoints and the public missing-result calculation all tie their
  competent conventional calculations. The earlier navigation test records a
  90 versus 89 Reiyah query disadvantage; the corrected native-grid test ties at
  69 each.
- The latest public simulation question remains blocked on cohort/provenance.
  Its narrower completion interval is ordinary exact missing-data arithmetic,
  and both arms agree. See [public decision](../../public-decision/0.1.0/README.md)
  at commit `948353a21872ebdf323e5c07a8f845124669a645`.
- There is still no genuine customer revision history, owner-defined release
  decision, measured reviewer/adjudication time, willingness to pay, repeat
  demand, deployment validation or independently authored replication.

These are not merely absences after one early prototype. They include multiple
direct comparisons, a negative reuse assay and corrected follow-ups. Continuing
to generate authored cases would test implementation correctness again, not the
investment claim.

## Correctness assessment

### What is established

For the implemented perception family, the exact statement is conditional on a
common weighted cohort, observed A and B outputs, one declared graph of eligible
detection-object edges per joint Boolean world, nonnegative false-positive and
miss penalties, and a fixed tolerance. Maximum one-to-one matching cardinalities
determine paired loss. Missing outputs block; open references produce conservative
bounds; inconsistent observation premises do not become vacuous support.

The audit variants add a declared reference-deletion or position family. A
universal result covers every admitted world only. Statistical procedures keep
their sampling error separate. The scalar motion work assumes interval samples,
a global Lipschitz rate, a single common clock offset and the stated interpolation
semantics. It does not establish that a vehicle or sensor satisfies those
premises.

Separate checkers reconstruct cardinality, bounds, response partitions or exact
sampling paths without importing producer verdicts in the checked studies. They
still share an author, strict parsing and parts of the semantic/runtime boundary.
They are not an independent team, physical calibration or external replication.

### What is not established

Actual data qualify only for each packet's narrow recorded-data scope. The
public perception labels are conditional benchmark references, the NGSIM result
is recorded-sample arithmetic, the ViF-GTAD export is not lossless, the mapping
study has a missing cohort member, and the latest Bench2Drive files lack complete
terminal/protocol evidence. No retained case supports a physical safety,
readiness, recovery or deployment conclusion.

## Decision value, competition and engineering utility

The proposed user is a validation lead deciding whether an old-to-new system
revision should advance. The consequential action, loss of a wrong decision and
cost of abstention must be supplied by that owner. None of the retained public
studies establishes that an actual team used the proxy result to gate a release.

Where Reiyah suggests a measurement, the complete-response study now correctly
recomputes the whole admitted scalar family. This closes the old pair-only
mechanism defect in that model. It does not show that the measurement is
physically available, calibrated, optimal or cheaper. Fully observed reference
families can still contain opposing decisions, so acquisition policy cannot
manufacture identification.

The strongest relevant baseline depends on the promise:

- For every-world finite guarantees: exact enumeration, robust optimization,
  maximum matching and an ordinary cached validation workflow.
- For statistical finite-population error: weighted confidence sequences and
  current active/prediction-powered testing with the same sampling access.
- For temporal signals: conventional LP/polyhedral projection and established
  robust temporal monitoring.
- For evidence custody: a strict manifest, schema validation, content-addressed
  cache and a small task-specific script.

Reiyah may eventually earn utility by integrating these obligations, but the
current engineering evidence is adverse: narrow adapters, finite caps, no
external consumer, no production integration, one slower reuse assay and no
matched complete-cost win. A hash proves byte identity, not truth, authenticity,
physical applicability or demand.

## Commercial assessment

A plausible buyer is an autonomy or robotics validation lead with recurring
model revisions, expensive adjudication and a release decision whose supporting
evidence is contested. This buyer and pain are hypotheses only.

No retained evidence establishes a buyer, budget owner, willingness to pay,
repeat demand, procurement path, adoption burden, permissible customer data,
measured reviewer saving, avoided failure or defensibility. Several public
source bodies have private-only or noncommercial/no-derivatives terms; public
access is not a product-data license. The 1,433 reserved images remain closed.

Scientific correctness in a supplied model does not answer commerce. The
business thesis needs a real workflow comparison, not more generated controls.

## The one warranted experiment and why it did not run

Only H1 supplies a decision-relevant next experiment: the already specified
[workflow comparison](../../value-disproof/0.1.0/WORKFLOW_BRIEF.md). It requires a
genuine chronological revision, an owner-defined criterion and action, complete
population and failure retention, the owner's best current scripts, an authorized
observation/adjudication process, and measured human and integration work.

No existing record qualifies. The public YOLO files are publisher generations,
not a team's revision history. The public mapping and simulation studies have
missing cohort/provenance records. Authored oracle replies are not reviewer work.
Outreach and new private-data ingestion are outside this task's authority.

**Experiment count is therefore zero.** The warranted experiment is `blocked`,
not silently replaced with another synthetic case, metadata inventory or replay.
This follows the task rule to execute only a defensible question within authority
and to preserve a boundary rather than invent activity. One plain missing-record
example is a release owner's timestamped statement that revision B would advance
only if metric M exceeds threshold T on population P, together with the actual A
and B outputs, adjudication events and the current baseline's timed work. No such
record is retained.

## Roadmap disposition

Keep:

- the small conditional matching/checking kernels and their tests;
- explicit missing, invalid, out-of-distribution and abstained states;
- the exact Hara counterexample as a possible narrow scholarly correction;
- immutable negative results, source ledgers and known failures.

Stop:

- another exposed selector sweep, authored fixture catalogue or endpoint solver;
- physical, simulation or recovery expansion without admitted inputs;
- claims of frontier superiority, commercial usefulness or a platform moat;
- treating repository scale, hashes, signatures or passing checks as value.

Replace the roadmap's immediate technical build sequence with one resume gate:
run the genuine workflow comparison above only after its required records and
authority exist. If the competent workflow ties or wins on correctness,
resolution and complete cost, archive the product hypothesis and retain Reiyah
as research infrastructure.
