# Keep the measured timeline and withhold the recovery label

Document ID: `reiyah.mand-timing.decision`. Version: `0.1.0`.
Lifecycle status: `exploratory`. 15 October 2026.

Continue with a narrowly supported observation: the selected manD P1/anger files
share every recorded time coordinate and expose distinct request, automation-state
and end-message boundaries. Do not admit a recovery label or readiness model.
The source clock is arithmetically coherent; the physical clock and outcome-monitor
semantics remain incomplete. No accident episode appears in the frozen scenario.

This is stronger timing evidence than a summary-only endpoint, within the specific
selected records. It does not establish dataset superiority, native temporal
precision, a common follow-up horizon or population coverage. The conventional
reference uses Fraction and SQLite on the same original files and agrees on all
rows, field summaries and event runs. No model comparison or work-saving claim
was tested.

The initial premise that metadata availability would lead to a complete outcome
record was not accepted in advance. The observed missing sensor prefix, continued
recording after the parking message and absence of a positive accident episode
show why availability alone cannot settle completeness or failure semantics.
A request clearing, manual-mode entry, scenario end and actual recovery are
separate events. None may silently replace another in an endpoint.

The next dependency is specific: seek release-linked capture/annotation evidence
for the physical time unit and latency, the rule generating EnvironmentalEvent=6,
and how monitoring and recording terminate. A positive accident control is needed
to check that rule; select any additional record under a new bounded protocol
without using it to estimate population performance. First inspect whether eligible
metadata or provided source identifies such a control. If semantics cannot be
qualified, retain the recorded request/state-transition observation only and
withhold recovery benchmarking. Do not repeatedly parse P1/anger, reopen TD2D,
expand unrelated streams or compensate with a readiness model.

The frozen owner budget allowed one archive, 32 direct capture calls, 1 GiB of
retained response bodies, 512 MiB of extracted output and a 2 GiB disk reserve.
The selected archive and two channels were acquired within those bounds. A partial
listing and failed literal filename lookup remain recorded. Prior sources, costs,
failures and closed comparisons are preserved. Source acquisition is closed for
this owner; publication/readback are separate. No outreach or private-data
collection is authorized by this packet. Gate A remains operator-unaccepted.
