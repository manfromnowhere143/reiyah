Golovin, Krause and Ray, [Near-Optimal Bayesian Active Learning with Noisy
Observations](https://arxiv.org/html/1010.3091v2), arXiv 1010.3091v2,
16 December 2013 (first submitted 15 October 2010), sections 2–3:
equivalence-class determination stops when the remaining hypotheses lie in
one decision class. Its Bayesian expected-cost guarantee assumes a specified
joint distribution and identifiable classes. Our inference is that query
selection should follow a check that the proposed tests can distinguish the
relevant decisions. We do not implement EC² or transfer its guarantee.
The arXiv nonexclusive distribution license does not authorize republication
here; paper bodies remain private.

Hongbo Wang, [Certified World Models as Sensing Clocks: Drift-Aware Deadlines
for Active Perception](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.01537v1), arXiv
2607.01537v1, 1 July 2026, sections 3, 5.4–5.5 and 6: its sensing deadline
depends on calibrated rollout error; full sensing assumes near-zero reset
error. It reports conformal comparators matching its learned short-horizon
validity/budget results and no clean partial-reset spectral advantage.
This is author-reported context, not replicated here. Our inference is that
scheduling an observation and qualifying its information are separate tasks.
CC BY 4.0 is recorded on the source; no paper content or code is redistributed.
