# Frozen measurement-contract comparison

Version 0.1.0. Status: exploratory. Parent outcomes were known when this
development follow-up was designed. No prospective or held-out claim is made.

Allocate all 623 unresolved current/union partial-timing rows in the checked
7,707-row operating table: 444 primary and 179 diagnostic-anchor rows;
529 current and 94 union rows. They use 542 filtered states and 689
state/family pairs. Exact allocation and 1,954 source bindings are retained
privately; no raw images, additional labels or motion metadata are opened.

Before new actual constructions, freeze source bindings, this method,
implementation, authored controls, runtime, supervisor and allocation. For
each allocated inherited endpoint, add only isolated rectangles for previously
absent unknown identities. Reuse a job only when image operands, known
references, full unknown census and inherited proof identity agree exactly.
Retain all outcomes, including failed/capped/incomplete jobs with inherited
evidence. No selector optimization or new uncertainty sensitivity sweep runs.

Limits: at most 2,000 distinct completion jobs, 128 known-plus-optional
references, cumulative 600 outer-process seconds for actual construction
and separately 600 for actual checking, counting failed/retried attempts.
The owned supervisor denies networking before offline runtime startup;
unreceipted commands prevent budget reset. Other phases have 120 seconds per
command. New research artifacts are capped at 128 MiB, checkout accounted
separately; stop growth at 5 GiB free space. Limits are ceilings, not targets.

Controls cover finite optional-world enumeration through budget three,
repeated geometry, zero budget, no predictions, tiny positive width, fixed
known references, unavailable inputs, identity/census violations, geometry,
padding with matching edges, altered parent worlds, and forged native proofs.
Validate the supervisor separately. Do not change frozen implementations to
erase a failure; retain attempts and issue a new freeze if correction is needed.

Success means checked opposite-decision worlds with identical complete
presence answers under this model. It does not mean real observations were
collected, all possible presence answers are useless, or a product advantage
was found. All 7,707 parent row identities and decisions remain intact;
2,199 blocked inputs stay blocked. Keep all 1,433 reserved images closed,
the historical controller unchanged, and Gate A operator-unaccepted.

Publication is limited to authored code, synthetic controls, source pointers
and reviewed derived summaries under their source terms. Individual prediction
operands, reference worlds, empirical native proofs and traces stay private.
Report full measured process costs with cutoffs; useful engineering seconds,
human observation times and dollar savings remain unknown.
