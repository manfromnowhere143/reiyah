# Stop this recovery-label route at the demonstrated evidence boundary

Document ID: `reiyah.mand-semantics.decision`. Version: `0.1.0`.
Lifecycle status: `exploratory`. 16 October 2026.

The inspected public manD source route is complete for this question. Keep its
recorded request/state observations; withhold recovery benchmarking and readiness
modeling. The new documentation does not close the missing event-generation,
clock-error or follow-up requirements. A planned failure/termination path cannot
be silently promoted into an observed physical-recovery endpoint.

The conventional comparison here is ordinary direct inspection of the same sources.
There is no new detector/model comparison or claim of reduced work. The useful
result is a source-admission decision: do not pay for another participant archive
in the hope that finding a marker will explain an unavailable monitor.

Reopen this manD recovery question only for materially new eligible evidence:
release-linked event/capture implementation or a complete annotation contract,
physical time interpretation and uncertainty, explicit termination/censoring rules,
and an identified positive ego-accident control. Inspect such evidence first; any
new record then needs a separately frozen, bounded protocol. Selecting a positive
control cannot estimate population performance. No claim of global source absence
or defective dataset labels follows from this scoped closure.

The next authorized mission step is to qualify a different public outcome source
at the source/protocol level before acquiring trials. Require a consequential
outcome question, a released event monitor or explicit annotation rule, clock
meaning, termination causes and coverage/censoring semantics. Compare the source
with the strongest applicable conventional analysis and stop if the outcome cannot
be separated from its proxy. A simulator criterion may support a simulator outcome;
physical recovery and human readiness require their own evidence. Do not begin a
new inference campaign or substitute automation deactivation for successful recovery.

Preserve all closed TD2D, P1/anger and microscopy work. No repeat acquisition,
unrelated sensor expansion, paid service or outreach is authorized by this packet.
The full HARBOR mission remains open. Broad product expansion remains stopped
pending demonstrated value, and Gate A remains operator-unaccepted.
