Diwan et al. study a fixed matroid with uncertain element weights, costly exact
reveals and certificates valid for all weights consistent with the replies.
Their offline verification setting receives true weights when selecting the
certificate. They give a polynomial algorithm and restricted online and
learning-augmented results. Reiyah's joint Boolean presence, conditional edges,
paired rank difference and physical acquisition uncertainty do not automatically
satisfy that model. A reduction and its assumptions must be demonstrated, not
inferred from shared vocabulary. The paper's publisher date is25February2026.

## Match the comparison contract first

A statistical comparison chooses a finite-pool or population estimand, a sampling
model, error probability and stopping rule. It measures false decisions,
abstention, labels and complete workflow cost over the frozen experiment.
Use conventional paired importance sampling and appropriate sequential inference
when that is the promised guarantee. Neither a variance optimum nor lower
variance alone proves the desired probability of selecting the best model.

A universal finite-model comparison instead requires the same decision for
every admitted remaining world. Both arms receive the same worlds, legal
measurement replies, matching semantics, costs and stopping certificate.
A conventional robust optimization or exact enumeration implementation is an
appropriate comparator. A future scalable solver must preserve this guarantee;
it cannot win by discarding difficult worlds or treating missing values as zero.

Neither setting creates physical uncertainty bounds from source hashes, nor
makes a logically revealing question obtainable. Count preparation, witness
checking, failed attempts, acquisition and integration alongside execution.
Logical reveals from already loaded data are not measured human effort.

## Bounded next engineering question

The current small kernel enumerates assignments even when all edges are fixed
and only object presence varies. Investigate whether incremental maximum
matching gain is monotone under adding objects, and whether that gives exact
bounds from the least and greatest feasible object sets in a guarded case.

Freeze the proposition and fixtures before execution. Require a direct argument,
an exhaustive independent small-graph calculation, actual current-kernel
agreement within its cap, and cases outside the cap with checkable endpoint
witnesses. Deliberately attack conditional edges, negative conditions, arbitrary
clauses, missing references and inconsistency. Reject the fast path when its
premises do not apply. Inspect existing consumer paths before any integration.

This is a proposed use of established matching/matroid structure to address a
specific implementation deficiency. It is not a new query algorithm, physical
experiment or competitor superiority claim. If the proposition fails, retain
the counterexample and terminate that fast-path hypothesis. If it holds but
does not improve a real engine limit within equal resource accounting, retain
parity and do not expand the implementation.
