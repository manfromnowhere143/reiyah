Document ID: `reiyah.multiorg.selected-cohort-next-decision`. Version: `0.6.0`.
Status: `proposed`. 14 October 2026.

The next dependency is reliable storage headroom. All three remaining source
images and their complete model inputs are qualified, but the first new pair
stopped at its disk reserve. The scientific endpoint remains incomplete. The
failure supplies no evidence for changing images, models, thresholds or references.

| Alternative | Decision |
| --- | --- |
| Establish storage capacity and stability, then freeze one new attempt on the same selected image | Recommended after resource qualification. |
| Lower the 4 GiB floor or remove retained evidence to make the run fit | Reject; it weakens the declared operational boundary. |
| Treat the checked partial prefix as a complete model output | Reject; missing tiles and the other model remain unknown. |
| Change the selected image or reuse a favorable partial result | Reject; changes the comparison after exposure. |
| Add a general research agent or a new detector | Does not resolve this execution dependency. |

The separate postmortem checker qualifies only persisted partial records. It must
retain the original complete-pair refusal, bind the supervisor and exact frozen
inputs, report any orphan or interrupted file, and avoid inventing a worker final
record or unseen forward count. No whole-image NMS or reference matching belongs
in this audit.

Before another empirical attempt, measure available and allocated storage and
reserve the complete worst-case retention budget above the unchanged 4 GiB floor.
Account for other activity on the shared volume. Retain free-space observations
at preflight, during execution and at a stop; the current supervisor records the
stop reason but not the exact triggering free-byte sample. A single successful
preflight is insufficient evidence of stable capacity. Do not infer the cause of
the space loss from one later memory or disk snapshot.

Use a new explicit resource plan and freeze for any later attempt. Preserve this
owner, the closed first pair and every earlier failure. The remaining three
model pairs still use the same fixed contracts. Do not stitch partial records into
a complete run or claim that prior work disappeared. The four-image matching
adapter is qualified on authored controls, but its empirical inputs must be frozen
only after all four complete pairs are admitted.

Acceptance remains all four complete pairs, all twelve fixed reference cases,
native/conventional/separate matching agreement, and exact global-versus-imagewise
composition. Stop on source, origin, arithmetic, geometry, coverage, cap or resource
failure. Retain a null or unresolved contrast without enlarging the cohort.

After that endpoint, assess whether the case justifies a prospective equal-
information complete-work trial. The ordinary matching census remains the
competent comparator. No demonstrated product advantage or scientific superiority
follows from the preparation, controls or partial audit.
