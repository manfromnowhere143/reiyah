# Research scope and retained sources

Document: reiyah.measurement-resolution.sources, version 0.1.0.
Research cutoff: 21 September 2026. Bodies and derived text remain private.

[Fu and Topcu, arXiv:1410.0083v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/1410.0083v1),
1 October 2014, integrates active sensing with reactive temporal-logic synthesis
under partial observation. It represents sensing as belief refinement and states
a condition on available sensors for its satisfaction guarantee. The primary
paper makes clear that actively obtaining information under temporal obligations
is established research, and that sensor availability matters to guarantees.
Our offline scalar response partition does not reproduce its controller, claim
its probability-one theorem or establish an advantage over its method.

Exact capture metadata, byte identities, access and distribution scope are in
[sources.json](sources.json). The public arXiv route was accessed without
authentication or bypass. No full-body redistribution grant is recorded; only
limited paraphrase and bibliographic pointers are published.

current Baird and Besset work on uncertain temporal decisions and informative
constraint tightening. Their retained findings continue to constrain novelty
claims. No new calibration evidence is inferred from those papers. The prior

model; [lineage.json](lineage.json) identifies the reused implementation and

not observations from ViF-GTAD, NGSIM or a private perception team. The closed
[nominal separation result](../../nominal-separation/0.1.0/README.md) and its

do not supply missing physical calibration, independent human adjudication or
