# Distribution scope, version 0.1.0

Only Reiyah-authored code, mathematical argument, authored inputs/results,
aggregate controls, source metadata and research reporting enter Git. New code
is covered by the repository's Apache-2.0 license. No third-party body, derived
article text, raw source trajectory, private receipt or private path-dependent
capture is included. Source citations retain authors and publication identifiers.
Their source-specific terms are recorded in sources.json, not replaced by the
repository license. Public distribution is not scientific acceptance.

Readback confirms exact distributed bytes from the publisher's session. It is
not independent transport verification, external replication, physical
calibration, a release decision or evidence of state-of-the-art superiority.
