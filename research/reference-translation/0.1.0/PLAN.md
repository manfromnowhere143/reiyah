# Localized reference translation on the frozen development cohort

Plan ID: `reiyah.reference-translation.development-plan`.
Version: `0.1.0`. Status: `exploratory`.

This follows the public-checkpoint comparison at
`d38a3ae6aa1e3d4f10bdda787dd80f1735a8e502`. Its outcomes are already known.
Freeze this plan and executable implementation before calculating the new
localized-translation curves. This is a declared follow-up on exposed data,
not preregistered outcome-reserved evidence. It does not revise the previous
protocol, query histories, exact query floors or broader adverse worlds.

## Question and fixed operands

What worst-case change in the existing replacement decision is possible when
the remaining reference error consists of small translations of existing
eligible rectangles? The broader arbitrary-edit analysis permits insertion,
deletion and unrestricted displacement. It does not establish whether its
counterexamples require large moves or different reference cardinality.

Reuse exactly the same 64 visible images, two frozen prediction exports,
64 supplied reference answers, 73 diagnostic cases, unit matching loss,
IoU threshold at least 1/2, height eligibility at least 25 pixels and strict
zero-tolerance improvement decision. Reuse the byte-bound predecessor packet.
No image or model membership changes, inference, acquisition, training,
reserved outcomes or human annotation are needed or authorized by this plan.
All 1,433 REC-D outcome-reserved images remain closed.

## Declared residual family

After a complete supplied reference answer, at most one existing eligible car
rectangle per image may be translated horizontally OR vertically by absolute
distance at most r pixels. Its width and height are fixed, the orthogonal
coordinate stays fixed, and the translated rectangle must remain wholly
inside the same image. It may overlap other references; matching remains
one-to-one. No insertion, deletion, class change, size change, simultaneous
two-axis displacement or movement of multiple references within an image is
admitted in this particular family. The unchanged answer is always admitted.

Edits across images may be correlated and adversarial. This is a narrower
declared stress set, not a measured error distribution or an asserted bound
on what a human reviewer, projection, calibration or physical sensor gets
wrong. Robustness here cannot replace the previous broader negative result.

Evaluate fixed radii **0, 1, 2, 4, 8, 16, 32 and 64 pixels**, inclusive.
Retain every allocated image/case/radius, failures and unresolved states.
Report full-information lower and upper deltas, decisions, attained worlds
and the minimum affected-image count capable of excluding a nominally
supported decision at each radius when it exists. No selector is tested or
tuned. Query savings and human time are not an outcome of this follow-up.

## Exact continuous geometry

For each reference and axis, partition the legal translation domain at every
exact IoU-edge interval endpoint. Include singleton endpoints and open cells
between them. The matching neighborhood is constant inside each open cell;
closed threshold equality is evaluated separately. Use exact rational
arithmetic on the retained numeric spellings, not rounded pixel samples or
a floating epsilon grid. A cell is reachable at radius r exactly when it
intersects the closed radius band around the original coordinate.

For a fixed cell, retain its infimum required radius and whether that radius
is attained. An open cell may become reachable only strictly beyond its
infimum. For the primary case, find the first radius in the declared interval
[0,64] at which an excluding world is possible, or establish that no such
world exists there. Distinguish an attained minimum from an unattained
infimum. For an unattained infimum, supply an actual rational radius above it
and an attained excluding world, and verify the endpoint's distinct result.

Native matching checks validate attained geometric witnesses. A separate
verifier reconstructs the complete partition through a piecewise-linear
overlap calculation, rather than reusing the producer's closed interval
formula. It checks coverage, edge constancy, matching extrema, radius
membership, edit cardinality, case compositions, minimum affected-image
counts and the primary critical-radius claim. Exactness is scoped to this
axis-translation family only.

## Bounded execution and reporting

Use at most 100,000 partition cells and 50,000 distinct matching states per
image, without raising the inherited Engine work limit. An exhausted work
limit is a retained unresolved analysis failure, not evidence of robustness.
Stop the search at its bound, retain all source/result identities and include
preparation, search, native proposal/check, verification and failed attempts
in known machine costs. Human work and full economic costs remain unknown.

Test synthetic cases before the actual search, including an IoU equality
endpoint, an adverse open cell with an unattained radius infimum, image-edge
clipping, a no-movement/full-width rectangle, shared and duplicate predictions,
empty references, correlated multi-image reversal and malformed edit budgets.
The source and synthetic controls stay public; actual reference geometry,
per-image proofs and translation-cell operands stay private. Derived tables
retain the predecessor nuScenes attribution and terms.

Any new implementation or scientific criterion after freeze requires a retained
amendment and a new run identity. A result contradicting an earlier bound must
be investigated and retained, not hidden by narrowing this plan after scores.
