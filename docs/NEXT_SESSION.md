## Mission and ambition

Build evidence that can change a consequential engineering decision.
The long-term ambition is a defensible technology useful to advanced autonomy,
automotive and robotics teams, with a credible path to an independent business,
technical partnership or licensing. Acquisition interest and valuation are
possible commercial outcomes, not technical acceptance criteria or established
facts. Do not substitute a prestigious company name for a user, decision or
measured benefit.

human-automation readiness, recoverability, joint silent misses, causal policy
effects, explicit unknowns, transfer and worst-group validation. The implemented
slice is conditional perception-revision analysis. Broader constructs remain
proposed or insufficiently validated. Reiyah is not a driver-monitoring classifier.

## Scientific standard

1. Trace implementation claims to code and execution paths, not specifications.
2. Verify current methods using original papers, official implementations and
   comparable protocols. Record exact versions, publication dates, licensing,
   compute and data requirements. Distinguish preprints from reviewed work.
   “2026” describes the review horizon, not a performance claim.
3. Choose a newer technique only when its expected benefit, integration cost,
   comparison and rollback criterion are explicit. Preserve a simpler method
   when the evidence supports it.
4. Keep observation, latent belief, decision, intervention, outcome and evidence
   distinct. Missing, invalid, unmeasured, abstained and zero are different states.
5. Separate a theorem under premises, a finite authored control, a saved-file
   replay, an empirical comparison and an operational claim.
6. Keep split independence, exposure, selection, incomplete outcomes and
   stopping rules visible. Retain failed attempts and complete denominators.
7. Challenge promising results with the strongest plausible conventional
   explanation. A method must meet the actual metric and correctness obligation.
8. Give every important conclusion an evidence path. Use “not established”
   where the evidence ends. Model agreement, test counts and polished prose
   cannot confer scientific authority.
