## Mathematical discipline

For the current additive FN/FP count loss, in one shared reference interpretation, with
nonnegative penalties a and b and r retained added detections:

```text
delta = loss_base - loss_augmented
      = (a+b) * (TP_augmented - TP_base) - b*r
0 <= TP_augmented - TP_base <= r
-b*r <= delta <= a*r
```

TP is the maximum same-class one-to-one matched count. Preserve every base detection. The
shared target count cancels under these specific assumptions. This conventional identity is
not a result about F1, latency, planner behavior or crash risk. Unknown objects near a base
detection can change the added channel's value through competing matches. Preserve the
counterexample, common opportunity population and shared cross-anchor interpretations.

Use exact source-decimal/rational comparisons for the declared boundaries. Numerical precision
does not establish physical calibration or timing. A matching and an equal-size vertex cover
certify the count optimum. The checker must not call the producer's matcher; their shared
parser and representation remain an explicit trusted surface.

An enclosure is conditional on its model and admitted reference alternatives. Unlisted matchable
objects and unresolved geometry/time/class effects require a justified relaxation or an open
bound. Do not truncate adverse worlds to meet a resource limit. A failed optional tight
calculation may retain a separately verified coarse bound; an invalid required operand leaves
the decision unevaluated. Inconsistent assumptions must not yield vacuous support. A wide
interval alone does not establish non-identifiability.

For every statistical or causal extension, name the estimand, population, observational unit,
selection rule, missingness mechanism, assumptions and target of generalization. Track clustering
by instance, scene or log where applicable; choosing one row per object does not prove independent
objects. Distinguish model enclosures, finite-population statements, sampling uncertainty and
causal identification. Do not label an interval as 95% physical coverage without the requisite
measurement and sampling justification.

## Choosing methods at the current frontier

There is no permanent list of techniques that is best for every use case. Use this decision
process when an unresolved technical choice could materially change correctness, utility or cost:

1. State the engineering decision and the exact computational or scientific obligation. Name
   the inputs, information available at decision time, assumptions, loss and unacceptable failure.
2. Establish the competent conventional method and the simplest sufficient implementation.
   Identify what it cannot do on this task before replacing it.
3. Inspect existing retained source material. For time-sensitive, unfamiliar or novelty-bearing
   questions, conduct a bounded current primary-source search. Record exact versions, dates,
   assumptions, scope, comparator and retained source custody when used as project evidence.
   Official documentation supports an API claim; a paper's stated evaluation supports only its
   demonstrated scope. Treat commercial claims with the same scrutiny as academic claims.
4. Compare the serious alternatives on validity, identifiability, required evidence, runtime,
   memory, integration burden and maintainability. Prefer the method that earns its complexity.
   If a relevant skill applies, read it and use its task-specific workflow; a skill is not
   scientific authority and does not replace operator instructions.
5. Attack the proposed method before evaluating it: a counterexample, independently structured
   calculation, tiny exhaustive population, boundary case or falsifiable experiment should
   expose its most consequential failure. Profile actual costs before escalating compute.
6. Record the choice and its limits briefly, then implement. Stop collecting literature when
   another source cannot resolve the remaining empirical question. Revisit the choice when a
   failure, changed assumptions or new evidence makes it necessary.

"State of the art" may describe a result only after a dated, relevant comparison establishes
it. A sound conventional proof can be the right component of a frontier system. No neural model,
solver, simulator, formal-methods tool or new infrastructure is prescribed for prestige.

Use different machinery when different obligations require it. Exact combinatorics addresses
the current bounded loss calculation; nominal transforms address recorded geometry; physical
reference review addresses observation uncertainty; appropriately designed inference addresses
sampling; an independent workflow comparison addresses usefulness. A passing tool cannot
silently substitute for a different obligation.

Maintain a research habit across substantive milestones. Use arXiv to discover relevant work,
then inspect the actual paper, its version, code or data where available, assumptions and
publication status. Follow primary work from relevant autonomy, robotics, AI, assurance and
statistics groups, including industry research and universities. Source quality and relevance
take precedence over institutional prestige. Official research from NASA, Mobileye, NVIDIA,
Waymo, Tesla, OpenAI, Anthropic or a university is a candidate source to examine, not a claim
that its method is superior or relevant to every task. Separate research evidence from a
company announcement. Do not treat a preprint as independently validated merely because it is
recent or its authors are prominent.

At each relevant review, retain a compact answer: what was learned, which assumption or
implementation it changes, what may transfer, and the smallest check needed before adoption.
Research should feed a proof, experiment, implementation improvement or an explicit rejection.
Do not substitute a growing reading list for a result. Trigger fresh reading when a new design
choice, failure, proposed novelty or consequential milestone warrants it.
