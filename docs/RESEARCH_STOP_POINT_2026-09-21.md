## Mission and present capability

HARBOR's proposed mission concerns object-level driver-vehicle belief,
human-automation readiness, recoverability, joint misses and causal policy
effects. The implemented perception engine addresses a narrower problem:
conditional revision decisions under explicit reference alternatives and losses.
It retains missing, inconsistent and unresolved states and emits checkable proof
payloads. The wider mission has not been achieved.

The [value verdict](../research/value-verdict/0.1.0/VERDICT.md) stops investment
in broad expansion because competent conventional methods repeatedly tie or
outperform the retained comparisons. The strongest conventional arm and Reiyah
use the same 3,279 total queries in the retained 20-case
[value study](../research/value-disproof/0.1.0/README.md). These are overlapping
development cases, not independent customer decisions. Complete workflow costs
and commercial demand remain unmeasured.

## Restart conditions for the product-value experiment

The existing [workflow brief](../research/value-disproof/0.1.0/WORKFLOW_BRIEF.md)
requires a genuine chronological old/new perception revision with:

1. complete opportunity membership, outputs and provenance for both versions;
2. an accountable owner-defined loss, release criterion and resulting action;
3. a qualified reference/adjudication process that keeps uncertain truth explicit;
4. the owner's competent conventional workflow, receiving the same information
   and meeting the same correctness and resolution obligations;
5. frozen comparison and stopping rules before outcome inspection, with complete
   preparation, review, retries, checking, repair and integration costs measured.

No retained record currently meets these conditions. Threefold fewer expensive
observations and halved complete cost remain unachieved investment targets.
Preserve a tie or disadvantage rather than changing the endpoint afterward.

An owner is the party accountable for the real decision and criterion. Daniel
does not need to invent domain expertise or already possess private team data.
A qualifying public record could be usable; ordinary public prediction files
alone do not supply decision history or measured reviewer effort. Sessions can
prepare and execute authorized technical work, but cannot invent a customer's
need or independent physical ground truth. GPU access does not fill this gap.

If the qualifying evidence and authority become available, start a fresh owned
candidate, freeze the prospective comparison and execute the bounded study.
If they do not, retain the stopped product hypothesis. Do not replace the missing
workflow with another solver, authored example, source inventory or exposed
parity sweep. Broader public or mathematical research requires its own concrete
question and scope; this stop decision does not make all such research impossible.
