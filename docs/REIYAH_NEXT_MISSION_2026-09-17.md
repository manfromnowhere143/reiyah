# Next mission and the evidence required to expand it

Document ID: `reiyah.next-mission`. Version: `0.1.7`. Lifecycle status: `proposed`.

## Decision

Build a decision-assurance engine that can reduce the work needed to justify a system change,
while exposing its assumptions and retaining unresolved outcomes. Perception reference audits
are the current test. The next milestone is an end-to-end comparative experiment with a common,
checkable stopping rule. Absolute strategic optimality, frontier superiority and acquisition
value remain unestablished.

The most useful distinction now is between choosing observations and proving that enough have
been obtained. The independent research lane's simple priority beat its guided selector on the
forty-frame case. The Engine now [checks several delivered sufficient sets](PERCEPTION_AUDIT_COMPONENTS_2026-09-17.md)
without trusting the selector or a mixed-integer solver's optimality claim. This lets competent
simple methods and ambitious new methods compete under the same obligation.

branch or restarting its session. The selected research deliverable now lives on

The Engine has since [qualified a REC✓D/KITTI development source](PERCEPTION_REFERENCE_CORRECTIONS_2026-09-17.md)
and supplied a 2D whole-image-alternative adapter. Two checked preference reversals show why
the existing deletion family needs an explicit boundary. The next external benchmark protocol
must prevent corrected reference sets from being visible before their query; the finite-alternative
adapter assumes they are already supplied. It is not itself a blind audit procedure.

The [deletion-margin proof](PERCEPTION_DELETION_MARGIN_2026-09-17.md) now checks exact
minimum adverse deletion counts and necessary confirmation bounds for all 1,125
supported census graphs. This removes solver optimality as a premise for those
specific quantities. It does not settle the remaining sufficient-audit, query-cost
or changed-system reuse questions. Its independent A/B contract is available for

preserved-base addition comparisons.

The [ordered-endpoint proof](PERCEPTION_MONOTONE_AUDIT_2026-09-17.md) resolves the

## Sequence and stopping gates

The [weighted split checker](PERCEPTION_SPLIT_CERTIFICATES_2026-09-17.md) distinguishes
an attained adverse set from a proved global minimum over differently weighted
scenes. Preserve any lower/upper gap, every unavailable member and all declared
weights. A pool optimum alone cannot justify a split-wide optimality claim. This
composition remains an exposed conditional loss calculation, not a new benchmark
metric, independent scene study or query-cost result.

The [localization checkpoint](PERCEPTION_LOCALIZATION_CERTIFICATES_2026-09-17.md)
now supplies a conventional geometric bound and exact displacement checker. The
forty-frame case is checked robust through 0.25 m under its stated assumptions;
0.50/1.00 m remain unresolved by that bound. Before comparing position-audit costs,
the research lane must repair its strict inner boundary and retain measured residual
uncertainty rather than freezing adjacency. A claimed realized counterexample must
supply its actual displacements; aggregate counts cannot substitute for them.

The [position-observation boundary](PERCEPTION_POSITION_OBSERVATIONS_2026-09-17.md)
now accepts source-bound returned centers and residual errors. It checks subject
applicability across output changes, retains partial intersection uncertainty and
rejects vacuous support from conflicting premises. Identical hypothetical observations
apply to two retained configurations, but the second decision stays unresolved and
the first proof cannot be reused. This supplies a common checking interface for the
research lane's histories. It does not establish avoided queries or economic benefit.
Consume it without restarting an already frozen experiment or changing that experiment's
answers, cohort, seeds or selection rules after results are visible. A new contract
requires a separately identified experiment version.

The [linear-certificate checkpoint](PERCEPTION_LINEAR_BOUNDS_2026-09-17.md) now resolves
the forty-frame 0.50 m case with no additional observations: lower bound 9/20 > 1/10.
The stronger common checker must be available to all selectors in the next experiment.
If it resolves a case before queries, that is a selector draw. The selected completed
cost/reuse return retains aggregate summaries, but not ordered answers or per-prefix
proofs. It cannot yet establish a native checked stopping point or reuse saving.
Preserve those runs; specify and freeze a new version before collecting full histories.
Across retained and fresh displacement replays, 375 of 376 supplied witnesses check;
one remains work-limited. No physical error frequency or query-cost advantage follows.

1. **Finish the common proof boundary.** Check complete local deletion coverage, shared global
   budgets, every compatible joint world and strict thresholds. An allowed counterexample under
   a claimed sufficient result falsifies correctness. The 373-label case is now checked by the
   endpoint method; keep remaining unresolved cases and do not transfer the theorem to general
   replacements or changed matching eligibility.
2. **Run the competitive development benchmark.** Use the same input bytes, error family,
   query unit, answers and stopping checker for random, geometric/adjacency, influence,
   counterexample-guided, direct matching and applicable conventional sufficient-set or
   hitting-set procedures.
   Run the cheap bound before a stronger proof when it already settles the decision. Preserve
   the costs of selection, preparation, loading, proof generation/checking and all earlier queries.
3. **Freeze a prospective external test before revealing its outcomes.** First qualify the input,
   split, reference semantics, selection rules, budgets, seeds, metric and cost policy. Current
   exposed scenes are development data. Reusing scenes or model outputs creates dependent cases;
   split and report at that dependence level. Existing public labels are a declared computational
   oracle, not independently audited physical truth.
4. **Measure changed-system reuse.** Compare the same valid procedure from scratch and with
   previously acquired, still-applicable observations, on a distinct output comparison. Recheck
   the current conclusion. Report initial cost, additional cost and amortization horizon. Changed
   loss weights alone do not establish reuse across a model change; witness overlap is not saved work.
5. **Expand only after a consequential win.** Extend beyond deletion errors when the selected
   source needs insertions, geometry or class corrections. Add another domain only with an explicit
   behavior/loss contract. A detector-loss certificate does not certify driving policy, spacecraft
   control or an LLM agent's permissions.

These are prospective requirements, not a claim that the already exposed development analyses
were preregistered. The next external selection and outcome-access boundary still need to be fixed.
No new physical study cohort, operator review, inference campaign or outreach is selected here.

## Benchmark scorecard

Report all of the following together; none is a substitute for the others:

- Invalid sufficiency certificates: every found counterexample is a failure, not an averageable score.
- Fraction of cases resolved correctly under the declared model, plus every unresolved or invalid case.
- Actual observation queries to first checked decision, with response types and query costs explicit.
- Complete measured computational cost and separately measured human effort when available.
- Additional cost after a change, alongside the cost of obtaining and validating reusable observations.
- Results against the strongest baseline, per case and dependence group, including wins, draws and losses.

For computational replay, report query-versus-compute tradeoffs before asserting money or hours
saved. If C_q is the per-query cost and T the measured computational cost, in the same units, a proposed method earns
an economic advantage only where `(Q_baseline-Q_method)*C_q > T_method-T_baseline`, with preparation,
integration and amortized prior work accounted for consistently. Unknown costs remain unknown.
A post-hoc certificate with 373 retained labels after 480 queries still incurred 480 queries.

General integer-programming proof systems are a real comparator. VIPR checks rational MIP
certificates independently, and SCIP 10.0 added exact rational solving and proof logging.
Reiyah's component argument is an application of established graph and dynamic-programming
reasoning; it does not establish novelty over those systems. Background sources and their access
limits are retained in the private checkpoint. See the [VIPR project](https://github.com/scipopt/vipr)
and the [SCIP 10.0 report, 23 November 2025](https://optimization-online.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/scipopt-100.pdf).

## When the next public result is ready

A technical update can describe the current bounded implementation and its limits. A headline
claim of reduced validation work needs a reproducible comparison on a frozen, distinct test
population, common correctness obligations, competitive baselines, complete costs and explicit
failures. A state-of-the-art claim additionally needs a dated, task-specific comparison with the
strongest applicable published methods. Tests and internal producer/checker agreement do not
constitute external scientific replication.

The current work is not evidence that Reiyah surpasses NVIDIA, Mobileye, Tesla, NASA or SpaceX.
The credible target is a particular validation task where a capable team gets the same justified
decision with materially less total work. A result of that kind is worth presenting to their
engineers. It cannot guarantee their reaction, willingness to buy, or a company valuation.
The operator will be told when that evidence exists; no post or outreach is sent by this plan.
