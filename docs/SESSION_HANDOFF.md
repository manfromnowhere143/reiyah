## Research operating model

The HARBOR research registry freezes the progression from question formation through evidence
admission, specification, preregistration, execution authorization, analysis, challenge,
replication, correction, and retraction. Generated prose, company disclosures, passing tests,
and consensus remain proposals or integrity signals. They cannot promote a claim.

The 2026 frontier review treats Tesla and Mobileye as bounded external comparators. Tesla's
company disclosures motivate tests of long-tail learning, low-visibility perception, inference
latency, supervision modes, telemetry denominators, and deployment boundaries. Mobileye's
company disclosures motivate tests of modular autonomy, cabin-road fusion, semantic failure
discovery, generated scenarios, formal policy claims, and integrated L2+ boundaries. Reiyah
claims no empirical advantage over either company. Its proposed contribution is a stricter,
independently falsifiable evidence contract.

All 38 frontier records are pointer only, evidence ineligible, and claim admitting false. A
future protocol cannot rely on them until exact permitted bytes, identity, rights, limitations,
and independent review are retained in an append-only successor.
