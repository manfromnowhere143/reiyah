## Scientific architecture established

Observation, latent belief, decision, intervention, outcome, and evidence remain separate in
identity, provenance, and time. Non-observed states remain typed as missing, unmeasured,
out-of-distribution, sensor-invalid, or abstained. None may silently become zero, false, normal,
or a confident label.

Five v1.1 application contracts cover:

1. object-level human and automation belief, frozen information sets, readiness, and recovery;
2. common opportunities, joint silent misses, selective prediction, conformal validity,
   transfer, and worst-group evaluation;
3. sequential off-policy evaluation with exact policies, propensities, support, estimator
   selection, uncertainty, and safety-cost estimands;
4. study design and preregistration with causal graphs, adjustment sets, access chronology,
   power, stopping, missingness, multiplicity, splits, and deviations; and
5. dataset, benchmark, ODD, scenario, test, hazard, argument, evidence, and change-impact
   assurance interfaces that explicitly confer no safety authority.

The scientific-contract profile classifies 461 reference and identity surfaces through 228
non-overlapping pointer classes. It adds 31 exact version bindings and three container-specific
membership bindings. The surface audit found no gaps, overlaps, shape mismatches, unreachable

## Research operating model

The HARBOR research registry freezes the progression from question formation through evidence
admission, specification, preregistration, execution authorization, analysis, challenge,
replication, correction, and retraction. Generated prose, company disclosures, passing tests,
and consensus remain proposals or integrity signals. They cannot promote a claim.

The 2026 frontier review treats Tesla and Mobileye as bounded external comparators. Tesla's
company disclosures motivate tests of long-tail learning, low-visibility perception, inference
latency, supervision modes, telemetry denominators, and deployment boundaries. Mobileye's
company disclosures motivate tests of modular autonomy, cabin-road fusion, semantic failure
discovery, generated scenarios, formal policy claims, and integrated L2+ boundaries. Reiyah
claims no empirical advantage over either company. It proposes an evidence contract designed
for independent falsification.

All 38 frontier records are pointer only, evidence ineligible, and claim admitting false. A
future protocol cannot rely on them until exact permitted bytes, identity, rights, limitations,
and independent review are retained in an append-only successor.
