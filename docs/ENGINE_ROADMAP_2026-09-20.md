# Reiyah research direction, 20 September 2026

Proposed roadmap version: **0.3.0**. Status: exploratory.
Research cutoff: 20 September 2026. This is a research choice under Daniel's
explicit expansion instruction, not an accepted safety or product release.

**Build an evidence engine that can say what an autonomous system's proposed
behavior establishes, what it contradicts, and which missing observation could
change the decision.** Keep the small perception proof kernel. Extend through
qualified relational motion evidence, then simulation validity and human
recoverability. The commercial hypothesis is that development teams need
defensible revision decisions and useful failure explanations across changing
models. Demand, full-cost savings and willingness to pay remain unmeasured.

The [primary-source review](../research/frontier-expansion/0.1.0/SOURCES.md)
covers current NVIDIA, Mobileye and SpaceX material, a limited Tesla review,
August 2026 VLA research and established temporal-monitoring mathematics.
It supports this direction as a testable choice; it does not establish that
Reiyah is state of the art or reveal any company's private engineering process.

This roadmap supersedes the scheduling guidance in the
[September 19 roadmap](ENGINE_ROADMAP_2026-09-19.md). Its findings and comparison
obligations remain. The expanded question does not require another perception
sensitivity sweep or claim that the unproven cost-reduction hypothesis succeeded.
No foundation-model training, simulator deployment or vehicle control is added.

## What to build around

| Evidence layer | Question Reiyah should answer | Present state |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Measurement | Which object, coordinate frame, reference point and time does an observation describe; what error remains? | Perception identity, geometry and position-observation machinery exists. Applicable motion/actor observations still need qualification. |
| Behavior | Does a proposed trajectory meet a named obligation in every admissible common scene, or is there a counterexample? | A new offline authored minimum-clearance slice is executed and checked. It is not a complete driving monitor. |
| Simulation | Would the release decision change under a different justified simulator or residual reality gap? | Proposed. Visual plausibility and matching model rankings are insufficient on their own. |
| Human–automation recovery | Does the joint state leave a measured recovery opportunity before a stated deadline? | HARBOR research scope. Gaze, attention, belief, intervention and successful recovery need separate observables and protocols. |
| Revision value | Does this evidence change an actual decision or reduce its complete cost against a competent workflow? | Unproven. Real revisions, owner-defined consequences and timed work remain missing. |

The connecting artifact should bind the model/revision, observations, uncertainty
family, typed obligation, exact decision scope, counterexample or certificate,
and all source limitations. A text rationale or confidence score can propose an
obligation; it cannot authorize itself as ground truth. No single score should
collapse perception correctness, consistency, safety, recovery and economic value.

## The first expansion is implemented

The [relational clearance packet](../research/frontier-expansion/0.1.0/README.md)
checks one declared continuous-horizon requirement: lead rear bumper minus ego
front bumper is at least a specified distance. It retains common-world actor
and clock alternatives, explicit interpolation and missing horizons. A separate
segment-pair calculation checks the producer's merged-knot calculation.

All 20 authored cases and 24 scene alternatives are retained: three supported,
seven contradicted, six unresolved, three blocked and one inconsistent-premise
case. There is exact parity with the equally equipped conventional calculation.
There are no real driving-performance, risk-reduction or latency claims.
The primitive is established mathematics; the useful extension is its explicit
evidence contract and its integration into Reiyah's conditional checking approach.

For example, a slowing ego vehicle can still close on a lead vehicle that slows
more sharply. Conversely, accelerating ego motion can preserve clearance when the
lead pulls away. Without the relevant lead trace, the correct result is unknown.
Safe endpoints also cannot prove what happened between them without a justified
motion model. These authored examples define tests, not estimates of prevalence.

The public Drive the Thoughts lead is concrete but not admitted as a clearance
benchmark: the inspected JSON scope lacks the required lead trace, snapshot
equivalence needs reconciliation, and underlying data terms remain to qualify.
No private perception-team release records are asserted available.

| 5. Establish repeatable revision value | Run the existing workflow brief with actual consequential old/new decisions, qualified observations, at least three chronological revisions when testing reuse, and measured preparation/review/repair costs. | Meet the existing prospective correctness and investment criteria, then repeat on a distinct population with external scrutiny. Stop the proposed economic advantage if competent parity or overhead erases it. |

The immediate next decision is gate 1, followed by one frozen comparison only if
the corpus qualifies. A wider monitoring framework, generated scenario factory
or learned world model should wait for that result. Public evidence qualification
can proceed without customer records. Customer-value claims cannot.

One missing-record example: to test “keep at least five metres from this car for
the next two seconds,” we need that car's identified rear-bumper trajectory,
the ego front-bumper trajectory on the same clock and road coordinate, and a
justified bound between samples. Ego waypoints plus a sentence saying “keep
distance” do not supply those measurements. Daniel need not possess them;
a qualified public source may provide them.

## Ambition that can earn a frontier claim

The intended differentiator is useful, inspectable decisions across uncertain
perception, motion and human–automation evidence. This remains a hypothesis,
not a moat or valuation. A frontier claim would need all of the following:

- A meaningful task and preregistered estimand, with independent reference or
  explicitly bounded identification uncertainty.
- Strong contemporary comparators under equal inputs, error obligations,
  inference access, abstention rules and full cost accounting.
- Distinct development and evaluation populations, scene/log dependence,
  complete worst-group reporting and enough events for the claim.
- Retained negative results, failures, excluded regimes and external replication
  or review commensurate with the proposed scientific or operational claim.

Current company directions suggest a relevant interface for this work: reasoning
and motion outputs, action-conditioned simulation, contextual human sensing and
explicit recovery conditions. They do not supply evidence that we can outperform
those companies. Do not make a broad benchmark claim from small authored tests,
and do not let a sophisticated model replace a missing observation.
