The archive's exact SHA-256 and Git blob identity match the retained source and

callable was executed. No correction outcome or selector result was recalculated.

The [paper's second arXiv version, 30 January 2026](https://arxiv.org/html/2508.06556v2),
provides additional context in Tables I–II and Appendix B.3. The authors report
measured microtask durations and derive costs using their annotator compensation
rate. They explicitly exclude manual duplicate-removal effort from the relevant
annotation totals. The methods retain ambiguity through repeated tasks and
aggregate soft labels; the released aggregate is not a record of each response.

These are useful annotation-procedure measurements. They do not establish total
work for a Reiyah-versus-conventional decision, and the omitted work cannot be
silently set to zero. A soft label also does not specify which future observations
are obtainable, their joint response possibilities, or how unresolved responses
affect the engineering decision. No criticism of the paper's stated task follows
merely because a different experiment needs more information.

## Current research informs the comparator, not the missing observations

Two other primary-source leads have different targets:

- [Coverage Aware Active Evaluation for Failure Discovery with Paired Systems,
  v1, 13 August 2026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.13719v1) allocates evaluations
  using a proxy system to discover target-system failures. Its stated target
  is failure discovery, rather than resolving a paired detector decision under
  uncertain references and measuring complete human work.
- [Active Evaluation of General Agents, v2, 10 February 2026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.07651v2)
  studies online ranking with agent/task selection. Its ranking baselines do not
  supply the missing detector-review records.

This is a bounded source-admission review, not an exhaustive novelty survey or
a reproduction of either method. Neither abstract justifies importing its
performance claims into Reiyah. An applicable conventional arm must receive
the same observations, uncertainty treatment and available mathematical tools.

## Concrete next dependency and stopping rule

The next experiment remains a **fresh, specified paired decision with an actual
reference-resolution process**. Before running it, bind:

1. Frozen outputs and the expected opportunity roster, preserving successful
   empty, missing and failed runs separately, with configuration provenance.
2. The decision, loss, tolerance, scope and residual uncertainty. A public
   retrospective question may proceed without a customer, but cannot establish
   that a real team saved work or made a consequential choice.
3. Actual review inputs and responses, stable pseudonymous reviewer/task IDs,
   procedure versions, unresolved outcomes and adjudication. Establish relevant
   dependence instead of assuming independent repeat answers. The finite
   [resolvability control](../../research/resolution-identifiability/0.1.0/README.md)
   is usable only if its observation premises are justified.
4. Complete work for both processes: preparation, acquisition, review, duplicate
   removal, adjudication, retries, checking, repair and integration. Retrospective
   mean task prices may be modeled explicitly but cannot stand in for this total.
5. A competent conventional comparator and exposure record frozen before diagnosis.

The original evaluator packet already requested in
[the submitted report](../maintainer-reports/evaluator-populations-2026-10-11.md)
would first support an empirical impact check of the reported software behaviors.
That remains distinct from a timed product-value comparison. The RF-DETR request
targets a genuine version-change case but still lacks the requested output packet.
One complete read of each existing comment endpoint on 11 October found no reply
after Daniel: evaluator zero comments, RF-DETR three ending with his request,
automotive zero. No message was sent by this investigation.

Check source identity against prior custody **before acquiring another archive**.
Require a concrete missing field and an information benefit. Do not respond to
absent evidence by another synthetic sweep, a new selector or a repeated exposed
comparison. Resume the relevant experiment when qualifying new records arrive;
otherwise preserve the unresolved dependency. No background monitor is running.
