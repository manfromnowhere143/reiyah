# Distribution boundary

Document ID: `reiyah.clock-alignment.distribution`. Version: `0.1.0`.

Publish original code, authored controls, exact identities and aggregate derived
findings. Keep raw CSV/MAT, workbook, PDFs, extracted text, archive ranges, HTTP
headers and complete per-row records private. Do not redistribute the author
paper under the dataset license; its retained copy is for private research.

Dataset attribution: Sarah Haas, Selim Solmaz, Jakob Reckenzaun, Simon Genser,
Virtual Vehicle Research GmbH, ViF-GTAD v2, 7 April 2023,
<https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.7808255>,
[CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
The retained license text and dataset metadata remain hash-bound. Our new
transformations are association constraints, aggregate counts and feasibility
certificates. They do not imply source-author endorsement or change their terms.

Normal guarded push and exact-byte publisher readback are authorized. They are
integrity observations by the publisher, not independent transport or scientific
verification, physical calibration, legal compliance or operator acceptance.
