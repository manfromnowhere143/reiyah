====================================================================================
DISCLOSURE SUFFICIENCY: what the silence histogram does and does not determine
three channels, 1 means silent, two populations of two equally weighted rows
====================================================================================
  population A: rows 011 100
    marginals            ['1/2', '1/2', '1/2']
    silence histogram    {1: 1/2, 2: 1/2}
    subset-averaged m_k  ['1', '1/2', '1/6', '0']
  population B: rows 010 101
    marginals            ['1/2', '1/2', '1/2']
    silence histogram    {1: 1/2, 2: 1/2}
    subset-averaged m_k  ['1', '1/2', '1/6', '0']

  every published summary identical: True

  the labelled query the two populations answer differently:
    population A: P(channel 1 silent) = 1/2, P(channels 1 and 3 both silent) = 0
    population B: P(channel 1 silent) = 1/2, P(channels 1 and 3 both silent) = 1/2
    adding channel 3 to an installed channel 1 removes all of its silence in A
    and none of it in B. The decision differs maximally on identical summaries.

  exact range of P(channels 1 and 3 both silent) over every population
  consistent with those marginals and that histogram:
    [0, 1/2]  width 1/2
    the Frechet interval permitted by the marginals alone is [0, 1/2]
    The probe lattice is a search, not the proof. The proof is that both
    endpoints are attained by the two populations above, and the Frechet
    bound caps the interval, so the range is exactly the Frechet interval:
    the histogram removes none of the ambiguity in this labelled query (True).

====================================================================================
WHAT A PORTABLE REPORT MUST CARRY, BY DECISION
====================================================================================
  average all-silent rate over k-subsets
      carry: silence histogram
      sufficient
  all-silent rate for one named subset
      carry: silence histogram
      NOT sufficient
  value of adding named channel j to installed set C
      carry: the two rates for C and C+{j}
      sufficient
  value of every candidate addition to installed C
      carry: one rate per candidate plus the rate for C
      sufficient
  every labelled subset query
      carry: all 2^K cell counts
      sufficient and minimal
  value of a channel never measured
      carry: a model, and its validity certificate
      no summary suffices

  For K = 5 the full labelled disclosure is 32 counts per stratum, which is
  smaller than the histogram plus marginals people have been tempted to ship,
  and it answers every labelled query. Compactness was never the constraint.

  DISCLOSURE BOUNDARY. Cell counts are not anonymous. A stratum holding n
  opportunities discloses the exact multiset of per-object silence patterns,
  and at n = 1 it discloses one object's pattern across every channel. Repeated
  releases of one population under different stratifications permit linkage.
  A minimum cell occupancy and a release ledger are therefore part of the
  contract, not an operational detail. Compactness is not privacy.
